    About this planner

    The Allied Health Framework for Value-Based Health Care (opens in a new tab) supports health professionals, managers and project leads as they work with consumers and communities to change the way care is delivered, so that it focuses on the outcomes that matter to people and communities.

    This planner turns the framework into a working plan for your project. For each of the framework’s five domains it gives you:

    1. Key questions to considerRecord your team’s answers to the framework’s questions.
    2. WorksheetsPractical templates for every element — from defining your population segment and mapping the care journey to costing, measuring outcomes and planning your evaluation.
    3. Guidance and examplesThe framework’s advice, tips, case studies and resources, where you need them.
    4. A project plan and executive briefPrint or save them as PDF to share with your team, consumer partners and executive.

    “VBHC is not about having everything in place before you begin, you can start anywhere. Just start!”

    Dr Deborah Cole, former CEO of Dental Health Services Victoria

    Start anywhere, and work as a team

    There is no single pathway through the framework: open any domain, answer what is relevant and mark the rest “Not applicable”. Download the project file to share your plan with your team and consumer partners — they can open it in this planner and keep building it.

    Your plan stays in this browser — the planner does not transmit anything. Do not enter information that could identify a patient or consumer.
