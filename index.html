Allied health framework tools

Interactive versions of three Queensland Health allied health frameworks, shared here for viewing and testing.

Each tool opens in a new tab, so this page stays open for you to come back to.

  • Draft for testing

    Research careers self-assessment

    Allied Health Research Careers Development Framework

    Rate your research capability against 61 statements across five domains, see your results on a chart and plan your development.

    About 10–15 minutes. Redesigned to match the other two tools; the questions and feedback are the same as in the tool published by Queensland Health.

    Open the research careers tool (opens in a new tab) About the framework (opens in a new tab)
  • Draft for testing

    Leadership self-assessment

    Allied Health Leadership and Advancing Practice Framework

    Identify the stage you are working within, rate yourself across six domains, see your Capability Compass and build a Work-Integrated Learning Plan with your supervisor.

    About 10–15 minutes.

    Open the leadership tool (opens in a new tab) About the framework (opens in a new tab)
  • Draft for testing

    Value-based health care project planner

    Allied Health Framework for Value-Based Health Care

    Plan a VBHC project across the framework’s five domains, with worksheets, guidance and case studies, then print a project plan and an executive brief.

    Work through it over time; your plan is saved as you go.

    Open the project planner (opens in a new tab) About the framework (opens in a new tab)

Before you start

  • These are test copies. This is not an official Queensland Health website, and the draft tools are not government policy.
  • Your answers stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, so other people cannot see what you enter.
  • Answers are kept on the device and browser you use. To share or keep your results, use each tool’s print or download options.
  • Please do not enter information that could identify a patient or consumer.
